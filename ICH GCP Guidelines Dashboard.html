The big picture

Purpose

GCP protects the rights, safety and well-being of trial participants and makes sure trial data are credible. If it wasn’t documented, it wasn’t done.

Who’s involved

Participant (subject), Investigator, Sponsor, IRB/IEC (ethics committee), Regulators, CRO and Monitor.

Three pillars

Ethics (Helsinki) · Science (sound protocol) · Data integrity (accurate, verifiable records).

Core documents

Protocol, Investigator’s Brochure (IB), Informed Consent Form (ICF), CRF, SOPs.

Key terms

Which version? ICH E6(R2) lists 13 principles and is the version most training uses. ICH E6(R3) was finalized in January 2025 and restructures GCP into 11 principles plus annexes, with more emphasis on proportionate, risk-based quality and fit-for-purpose data governance. Serious Adverse Event (SAE)

Any untoward medical occurrence that, at any dose: results in death, is life-threatening, needs hospitalization or prolongs it, causes persistent or significant disability, or is a congenital anomaly/birth defect. Investigators report SAEs to the sponsor immediately.
